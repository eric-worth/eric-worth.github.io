PRODUCT MANAGEMENT · ENGINEERING · DATA

I like figuring out how things work.

THE POINT

Find the question that could change the decision.

Product work is not asking every possible question. It is finding the uncertainty that matters most before it becomes expensive.

ABOUT

A product manager who likes understanding how things work.

I’ve spent most of my career somewhere between the customer and the people building the thing.

I’ve worked on software, physical products, large deployments, data, cybersecurity, and everything that tends to get attached to those projects. Sometimes my job is defining requirements. Sometimes it’s figuring out why something failed. Sometimes it’s getting five people in a room to agree on what we’re actually trying to do.

I don’t need to be the person with all the answers. I do want to understand the problem well enough to ask good questions, spot the important constraints, and help turn a vague idea into something real.

Outside of work, I like family, the outdoors, cars, gear, and taking things apart to see how they work.
